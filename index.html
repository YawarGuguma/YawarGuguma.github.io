01 / ABOUT

Research at the intersection of vision, time, and trust.

I am a Research Professor at Kyungpook National University in South Korea. My work spans computer vision, deep learning, temporal action localization, and reliable learning from limited supervision.

Before KNU, I worked in industrial AI R&D as a Principal Researcher and AI Team Lead at OnDAI, following a long academic career in electronics, computer systems, and artificial intelligence.

02 / RESEARCH SYSTEM

From sparse evidence to reliable temporal understanding.

My current research asks how much a model can learn from minimal annotation — without pretending every annotation is equally reliable.

01 / INPUT

Sparse Supervision

Human points, weak labels, limited temporal evidence.

02 / REASONING

Trust Modeling

Estimate when supervision is informative, ambiguous, or unreliable.

03 / TEMPORAL

Action Localization

Convert sparse cues into temporal support and action proposals.

04 / OUTPUT

Reliable Video AI

Accurate localization with supervision quality made explicit.

RESEARCH AREA

Point-Supervised Temporal Action Localization

Learning action boundaries from sparse human clicks rather than dense temporal annotations.

RESEARCH AREA

Trustworthy AI

Reliability-aware learning, uncertainty, robustness, and out-of-distribution behavior.

RESEARCH AREA

Computer Vision

Object detection, small-object detection, image understanding, and visual perception.
